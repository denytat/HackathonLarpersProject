Methodology and Validation

Swarmspotter evaluates detection performance against a balanced test set containing both coordinated bot campaigns and organic viral look-alikes. By combining Layer 1 burst metrics with NVIDIA Nemotron 3.5 Super VL technique classification and Google Gemini 3.8 Flash cross-checks, our agreement rule successfully minimizes false positives on benign activist and fandom spikes.

hub
Layer 1: Deterministic Network Burst Engine

Handles posting-time bursts and account similarity graphs (Python, scikit-learn, networkx — deterministic & measurable). Computes sliding-window dispersion indexes and cross-account hash similarity across seconds-level windows.

psychology
Layers 2 & 3: Multimodal Disinformation Reasoners

Layer 2 and 3 models provide technique classification and long-context cross-checks (NVIDIA Nemotron 3.5 Super VL vision + Google Gemini 3.8 Flash search grounding) to isolate deliberate syntactic templates, visual manipulation, and manufactured memes.

rule_folder
Strict Tri-Partite Agreement Rule

Agreement rule requires both swarm signals and model verdicts to align before flagging. Prevents wrongful flags; non-aligned cases remain ‘Uncertain’ and are quarantined to a forensic human-in-the-loop review queue rather than penalized outright.
