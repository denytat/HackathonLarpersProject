PLATFORM ARCHITECTURE & MISSION

Transparent Intelligence Against Coordinated Manipulation

Modern influence operations rarely rely on naive copy-paste bots. They blend LLM-paraphrased messaging, staggered timing windows, and multi-tier relay rings. Swarmwatch turns opaque social streams into auditable forensic evidence.

Four-Stage Detection Methodology
How Swarmwatch isolates coordinated swarms without flagging organic viral discourse
01 // INGESTION

Stream Normalization

Validates schemas, aligns UTC timestamps, extracts entity mentions, URLs, and reply chains from heterogeneous social exports.

02 // MULTI-MODAL SIGNALS

Temporal & Semantic Fusion

Measures inter-post burst anomalies alongside dense semantic embeddings to catch paraphrased talking points.

03 // GRAPH FORENSICS

Swarm Community Detection

Constructs weighted account-similarity graphs and isolates high-density clusters (seeders, amplifiers, relays).

04 // EXPLAINABILITY

Evidence-Backed AI Synthesis

Generates human-readable verdicts, highlights manipulation tactics, and links every claim to verifiable post evidence.

Analyst Guardrails & Responsible Design

Swarmwatch is built around behavioral coordination, not political viewpoint filtering. Organic grassroots trends can exhibit high volume, so Swarmwatch requires converging multi-modal evidence—simultaneous timing synchronization, structural account similarity, and template alignment—before escalating an alert.

Every verdict exposes raw similarity pairs, cluster topologies, and timeline anomalies so trust & safety teams remain the final decision-makers.
